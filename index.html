About

A short snapshot of who I am and what I do.

I’m Harkanwar “Shark” Maan — an Electrical Engineering student and Services Engineering Cadet at BESIX Watpac. I work on major design-and-construct projects like the Woolworths Sydney Fresh & Chilled Distribution Centre, following my earlier involvement on the Barangaroo Metro Station project. My day-to-day blends engineering, coordination and problem-solving across electrical, mechanical, hydraulic and fire packages.

Outside of construction, I build software tools that improve workflow efficiency — from subcontractor rating systems, to payroll automation engines, to engineering simulations and data-driven models. My focus is simple: build things that solve real problems. Whether it’s designing a commissioning process, optimising a services package, or coding a tool from scratch, I enjoy taking ideas from concept to something tangible, functional and used.
