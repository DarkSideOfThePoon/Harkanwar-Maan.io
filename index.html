About

Engineering judgement with builder energy.

I like work that has consequences: live projects, real stakeholders, hardware, deadlines, constraints and systems that need to perform outside a classroom.

I’m Harkanwar Maan, an Electrical Engineering and Computer Science student at UNSW and a Services Engineering Cadet at BESIX Watpac. My day-to-day work spans electrical, mechanical, fire, hydraulic, security, vertical transport, refuelling and commissioning coordination on major design-and-construct projects.

The pattern across my work is simple: I find operational friction, understand the technical details, then build something useful around it. That might be a commissioning tracker, an automation engine, a Python model, an embedded prototype or a cleaner site workflow.

I’m especially interested in construction technology, automation, embedded systems, data-driven decision tools and the overlap between engineering delivery and investment thinking.

What I bring

  • Full-time project exposure while completing engineering study
  • Services coordination across multiple building systems
  • Strong communication from sales, site and subcontractor-facing work
  • Software mindset: automate, measure, simplify and document
  • Comfort moving between technical detail and commercial context
